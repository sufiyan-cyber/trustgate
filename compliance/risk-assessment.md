# Risk Assessment

## Classification
- **Risk Tier**: High
- **System**: Trust Gate Cyber-Physical Identity & Access Control Agent

## Justification
Trust Gate governs physical entry turnstile actuation and processes sensitive biometric and government identification credentials. Consequently, it is classified as a high-risk system requiring conditional human-in-the-loop escalation, strict segregation of duties, one-way SHA-256 credential hashing, and immutable audit logging.
